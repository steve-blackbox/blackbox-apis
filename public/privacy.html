1. Zero Persistent Data Footprint

Our backend infrastructure operates entirely on a database-less model. No persistent transaction logs, payload histories, or user identity maps are stored or written to local hardware disks. All dynamic data vectors run purely inside volatile sandboxed system memory.

2. Third-Party Payment Isolation

All commercial transactions and customer e-mails are handled directly over secure pipelines encrypted by the Stripe Merchant Core billing network, maintaining strict data decoupling boundaries away from our internal codebases [23context].
